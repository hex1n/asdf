# e2e-test-planner skill eval — rounds 1–4 (2026-07-12)

Four pre-registered blind-graded rounds measuring the e2e-test-planner skill on three synthetic fixtures (S1 e-commerce coupon, S2 SaaS export, S3 IoT firmware rollout), each fixture carrying 10 planted risk keys. Arms generated by Codex gpt-5.6-sol fresh contexts reading the working-tree skill files; graders blind (Plan A/B, recorded coin flips), rounds 1–2 Claude graders, rounds 3–4 Codex gpt-5.6-sol medium. Grading raws, anonymized arm texts, and pre-registrations lived in the session scratchpad (transient); the verdicts are archived verbatim below.

- Round 1: old skill vs noskill, standalone-document rubric — skill lost coverage 0/3.
- Round 2: fixed skill (P0 expansion duty, Probe/Oracle column, concurrency pair enumeration) vs noskill — parity within grader noise; fix beat old skill 3/3 cross-round.
- Round 3: fixed vs noskill under the handoff-consumption rubric (executor perspective) — coverage still parity; skill's decisive value is operational structure (0 safety-classification gaps vs 67; 5 vs 51 missing dependencies).
- Round 4: old vs fixed head-to-head, same rubric and grader, archived texts, zero confounds — **fix delta confirmed** (coverage strict wins 3/3, inventions 11 ≤ 13), mechanism-attributable.

## Positioning boundary (standing, evidence-backed)

The skill buys **operational handoff structure at parity coverage and ~2x document size**: safety classification on every mutating scenario, derivable execution order, systematic gap-routing/honesty (inventions consistently lower than noskill), zero filler in expanded form. It does not buy standalone-document completeness or raw planted-risk coverage above a strong bare model. Do not optimize it toward "more coverage"; optimize the executor-facing structure and the honesty rules.

Known residual defects after round 4 (round-5 targets): audit/idempotency contract assertions invented in oracle positions when fixtures define no such contract (triple-confirmed); inventory rows outside the DAG counted as missing dependencies; perf scenarios without thresholds judged waste; K9-class (mixed-version population) model-blind in every arm, every round.
